# Ownership baseline for a 2013 L405

Use this to create a high-value initial health check for an owner of a used vehicle.

## First-week baseline
- Verify VIN, engine, build date, market specification and recall status.
- Obtain a full JLR-capable scan and save the report before clearing anything.
- Load-test the 12 V battery; verify charging and inspect primary earths/terminals.
- Check cold coolant level over several mornings and photograph the reference level.
- Inspect for dried coolant residue and any evidence of prior overheating.
- Check all four air-suspension corners after an overnight park; note compressor behavior.
- Verify all four tyres are correct size/spec, similar wear, correct pressures and not severely mismatched.
- Inspect under carpets/load space for dampness and signs of past water ingress.
- Exercise every door latch/soft-close function and verify open recall status.
- Test parking brake, cameras, infotainment, seat/SRS functions, climate control, heated/cooled features and powered tailgate.
- Record engine cold-start sound and hot-idle sound as future comparison references.
- Gather service invoices; do not rely on dashboard service reset alone.

## Preventive mindset
The most expensive L405 failures often become expensive because an upstream condition is ignored: coolant loss becomes overheating; an air leak kills a compressor; a weak battery creates network chaos; water ingress becomes module corrosion; mismatched tyres stress driveline clutches.
