# MY2013 L405 powertrain map

Use this file to prevent cross-engine diagnosis.

## Launch-era engines (market dependent)
- 5.0L supercharged petrol V8 (AJ133): major launch petrol engine; cooling-system water pump history, supercharger isolator noise, timing-drive concerns on the broader AJ133 family, brake-vacuum-hose recall on affected supercharged vehicles.
- 3.0L supercharged petrol V6 (AJ126): introduced in some markets around the launch period; shares architecture with AJ133 but do not assume every V8 failure applies unchanged.
- 3.0L TDV6/SDV6 diesel: market-dependent; EGR/DPF/boost/intake and lubrication history must be kept distinct from petrol engines.
- 4.4L SDV8 diesel: market-dependent; cooling fan recall exposure on some MY2013 vehicles, turbo/intake/EGR/oil-cooler and ancillary leak patterns differ from V6 diesel.

## Hard rule
Never give an exact coolant capacity, engine-oil specification, belt layout, spark/glow-plug instruction, torque, part number, or engine-specific leak diagnosis until engine and market are known.

## Identification inputs
Prefer in order:
1. VIN/build data.
2. Registration/vehicle card showing engine/fuel.
3. Engine-bay photo.
4. Displacement + fuel type stated by owner.

For UAE/GCC vehicles, do not assume a UK or North American derivative from trim name alone.
