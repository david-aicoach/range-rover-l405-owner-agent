# Recall, SSM and TSB intelligence — launch-era L405

This is a curated index, not a substitute for VIN-specific recall lookup in the owner's market.

## High-value manufacturer/service items

### AJ133 5.0 V8 water pump — SSM64677
JLR documented coolant leaks from the AJ133 water pump and stated that multiple pump changes were made to improve quality. For Range Rover, SSM64677 cites improvement monitoring around VIN LG120564 and points technicians to LTB00352 for diagnosis.
Source: https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2013/MC-10206491-9999.pdf

Diagnostic implication: on an early 5.0 SC with falling coolant, dried coolant residue around the front of the engine or pump area deserves high ranking even when no puddle is present.

### Electrical earth fixing — SSM67737
JLR documented loose earth fixings/body studs on L405/L494 causing varied electrical symptoms, including multiple warning lights, instrument-pack problems and display faults. The primary battery earth G4D178 is explicitly mentioned.
Source: https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2013/MC-10206608-9999.pdf

Diagnostic implication: a cluster of apparently unrelated electrical faults can be one supply/ground fault, not multiple failed modules.

### Battery/charging warning with U0447-00 — SSM68518
JLR documented battery warning/charging-system messages linked to gateway communications; the action included checking main battery earth G4D178 before deeper diagnosis.
Source: https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2013/SB-10100969-9340.pdf

### Air-suspension compressor thud — SSM64937
A light thud from the rear load-space area shortly after startup/height achievement may be normal compressor exhaust-valve operation, not a fault.
Source: https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2013/MC-10206493-9999.pdf

### Dynamic Response air lock — SSM64917
JLR documented specific Dynamic Response DTCs that can be caused by air trapped in the hydraulic circuit. The manufacturer instructed technicians to bleed/test before electrical continuity checks or component replacement.
Source: https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2013/MC-10206492-9999.pdf

Diagnostic implication: some chassis codes are hydraulic-state problems, not necessarily failed electronic parts.

### A/C compressor run-in — SSM69318
JLR warned that replacement A/C compressors could fail prematurely if not run in correctly because of insufficient early-life lubrication.
Source: https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2013/MC-10206634-9999.pdf

Diagnostic implication: after an A/C compressor replacement, early failure can be a procedure/history issue rather than simply another bad compressor.

### 5.0 SC supercharger clatter/knock/rattle — LTB00482NAS6
JLR identifies torsional isolator / spring support shaft wear causing backlash in the 5.0L supercharger drive on affected L405 vehicles.
Source: https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2016/SB-10106301-9340.pdf

Diagnostic implication: a front-engine clatter is not automatically timing-chain noise. Isolating the supercharger drive is a discriminating workshop test.

### Door latch safety history
MY2013-2016 L405 vehicles were involved in recalls/investigations for doors appearing closed but not securely latched. NHTSA campaign 15V385 and later campaigns/investigations addressed the keyless vehicle latch system; subsequent recall work was itself re-addressed for some vehicles.
Sources:
- https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls (VIN-specific lookup)
- https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/inv/2018/INIM-EA18004-74558.pdf
- https://www.automotivesafetycouncil.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/NHTSA-Recalls-of-Interest-July-13-2015.pdf

Diagnostic implication: any door that appears shut but bounces, intermittently will not latch, or has a history of unlatching is a safety issue, not a nuisance lock fault.

### Supercharged petrol brake-vacuum hose recall
Affected L405 V6/V8 supercharged vehicles had a recall for brake vacuum hose routing that could chafe against an auxiliary drive pulley and reduce brake servo assistance.
Cross-check by VIN/market before asserting applicability.

### MY2013 diesel cooling-fan-module fire recall (some markets)
Some MY2013 TDV6/SDV8 L405 vehicles were recalled because a cooling fan module internal short could lead to cable insulation combustion/fire propagation. Cross-check VIN/market before asserting applicability.

## Recall behaviour
For any safety recall question:
1. identify market and VIN,
2. use the official national recall checker/JLR retailer,
3. distinguish campaign existence from confirmed applicability,
4. never infer completion status from vehicle age.
